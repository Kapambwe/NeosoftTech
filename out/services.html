What We Build

Our Services

AI-driven platforms for governance, finance, agriculture, mining, and SME development\u2014turning daily activities into trusted data for productivity, accountability, and inclusive growth.

Economic Systems, Not Apps

Our platforms change how economies function—how trust is built, how risk is measured, and how capital is allocated.

Data as Proof of Performance

We convert real operational activity into bankable, auditable data that unlocks finance and accountability.

AI-Driven Risk & Trust Scoring

Corruption Risk Indexes, SME performance indexes, and supply-chain risk models embedded directly into workflows.

Public-Private Collaboration

Governments gain visibility. Businesses gain trust and access to finance. Investors gain measurable risk and return.

Digital Government & Councils

End-to-end digitisation of local government operations—financial management, procurement analytics, audit trails, and real-time service delivery dashboards.

  • Council revenue, markets, and asset management
  • Ministry oversight and performance monitoring
  • CDF lifecycle management and tracking
  • Municipal bond readiness and fiscal transparency
  • Government auditing and compliance platforms

SME & Investment Platforms

Data-driven platforms that transform informal small businesses into investable, export-ready enterprises connected to capital markets.

  • Small-scale business management and clustering
  • Business extension services and quality control
  • SME performance indexes and ETF-ready metrics
  • AI-enabled investment pipelines
  • Ministry-level trade and industrial policy dashboards

Agriculture & Food Systems

Farmer, extension, logistics, and traceability platforms powering productivity, financing, and national food security targets.

  • Farmer management (crops, livestock, irrigation, finances)
  • Extension officer field visit and advisory tracking
  • National animal identification and traceability
  • Ministry coordination for import substitution and export growth
  • Consent-based financial inclusion (collateral-free lending)

Mining & Natural Resources

Artisanal and industrial mining traceability, mineral supply-chain transparency, and export monitoring platforms.

  • Mining regulator licensing and compliance tracking
  • Gold aggregation and traceability (ZCGC)
  • Digital minerals trading platforms
  • Production and export monitoring
  • Investor confidence and market transparency tools

Financial Crime & Risk Analytics

AI-powered anomaly detection, network analysis, and investigation platforms to detect corruption, procurement abuse, and illicit financial flows.

  • Graph and network analysis for financial crimes
  • Corruption Risk Indexes and scoring models
  • Support for ACC, DEC, and Financial Intelligence Centre
  • Procurement abuse detection
  • Anti-money laundering and compliance analytics

Land, Justice & Citizen Platforms

Digital infrastructure for customary land governance, national land audits, citizen services, and access to justice.

  • Chiefdom customary land application systems
  • Land audit and encroachment monitoring
  • Citizen digital portals (documents, e-services)
  • Courts and legal case management (e-filings)
  • Police case management and investigation tracking

Water, Forestry & Environment

Platforms for sustainable natural resource management, environmental protection, and climate goal tracking.

  • Lake, dam, river, and wetland monitoring
  • Forest reserve management and deforestation tracking
  • Afforestation programme monitoring
  • Environmental compliance and reporting

Education & Health

School and health facility management platforms that improve service delivery, accountability, and national development outcomes.

  • School management (operations, bursaries, scholarships)
  • Ministry of Education national performance dashboards
  • Clinical workflows and patient record digitisation
  • Hospital administration and facility oversight

Accounting & Financial Services

Standardised accounting infrastructure and banking platforms that operationalise “data as collateral” for inclusive finance.

  • National accounting infrastructure for SMEs, councils, and institutions
  • Auditable financial trails (invoices, receipts, approvals)
  • Banking and lending product management
  • Loan origination using verified activity data
  • Consent-based data sharing for credit scoring
