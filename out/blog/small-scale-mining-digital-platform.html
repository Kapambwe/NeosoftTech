Small Miners Digital Platform: Formalising, Financing & Scaling Zambia's Mining Sector from the Ground Up

NeoSoft TeamMar 17, 202615 min read

Zambia sits on one of the world's richest mineral belts — copper, cobalt, emeralds, gold, manganese, and dozens of industrial minerals lie beneath its soil. Yet the thousands of artisanal and small-scale miners (ASM) who dig this wealth daily remain largely invisible to the formal economy: unlicensed, unbanked, and unconnected to the global markets that pay premium prices for the minerals they extract.

The Small Miners Digital Platform changes this. It is a comprehensive digital ecosystem that formalises the entire small-scale mining value chain — from claim registration and production tracking to financial records, compliance management, and direct market access — unlocking opportunity for small, medium, and large-scale miners alike, and building lasting wealth for local communities.

The Scale of the Opportunity

Zambia's mining sector is one of the most significant on the continent. Yet the artisanal and small-scale mining (ASM) sub-sector — which employs an estimated 500,000 to 1.5 million people directly and indirectly — remains largely informal, fragmented, and excluded from the investment and financing that would allow it to grow.

Why ASM Miners Cannot Scale Without Digitalisation

The barriers facing small-scale miners are not a lack of minerals or motivation — they are structural and systemic:

  • No formal registration or verifiable identity as a legitimate miner
  • No production records, making it impossible to prove track record to investors
  • No financial statements or bookkeeping, blocking access to loans and insurance
  • No direct market access, forcing miners to sell below value to middlemen
  • No safety or environmental compliance record, increasing operational risk
  • No community governance structure, excluding villages from mining revenues

The result is a vicious cycle: miners cannot prove their value, so they cannot access capital; without capital, they cannot invest in safety, productivity, or compliance; without compliance, they cannot access premium markets or government support. The platform breaks this cycle permanently by turning every day of mining into a trusted, verifiable digital record.
