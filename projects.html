Our Portfolio

Projects

We are building a national-scale, data-driven digital ecosystem designed to transform agriculture, SMEs, mining, land management, local government, and public finance in Zambia and beyond.

Our platforms are cloud-first, mobile-first, and offline-capable, converting daily activities into trusted data for productivity, accountability, financial inclusion, and policy delivery.
