About Us

Digital Economic Infrastructure for Africa

NeoSoft Investment Ltd is a technology and data-analytics company focused on building AI-driven platforms for governance, finance, agriculture, mining, and SME development.

Unlike traditional software vendors, NeoSoft does not build isolated applications. We build integrated digital ecosystems that connect:

  • Government systems (councils, ministries, regulators)
  • Small businesses and producers (farmers, artisans, miners, SMEs)
  • Financial institutions and capital markets
  • Development partners and regulators

Our Approach

What Makes NeoSoft Different

Economic systems, not apps

Our platforms are designed to change how economies function—how trust is built, how risk is measured, and how capital is allocated.

Data as proof of performance

We convert real operational activity (production, sales, deliveries, compliance) into bankable, auditable data.

AI-driven risk and trust scoring

We embed Corruption Risk Indexes, Social Credit Scores, SME performance indexes, and supply-chain risk models directly into workflows.

From development finance to capital markets

Our systems enable a natural progression: grants and blended finance, concessional lending, SME mutual funds and ETFs, and pension-fund and capital-market participation.

Built for public-private collaboration

Governments gain visibility and control. Businesses gain trust and access to finance. Investors gain measurable risk and return.

What We Build

Core Platform Domains

SME & Investment Platforms

Mission

To build data-driven digital public and market infrastructure that enables transparency, trust, and scalable economic participation for governments, small businesses, and citizens across Africa.

We design and deploy AI-enabled platforms that transform fragmented, informal, and opaque systems into verifiable, auditable, and investable ecosystems—unlocking affordable finance, improving governance, reducing corruption, and accelerating inclusive growth.

Vision

To become Africa’s leading builder of digital economic infrastructure—where every council, small business, farmer, miner, and institution is digitally visible, trusted, and connected to capital markets.

We envision economies where small businesses replace imports and grow exports through software-enabled scale; governments make decisions using real-time data, not estimates; capital flows to productivity, not privilege; and transparency lowers risk, reducing the cost of capital—creating local prosperity and reducing migration to Europe and other continents by unlocking opportunity at home.

In this future, software is not just IT—it is economic infrastructure.

Values

Our core values guide how we build, partner, and deliver impact across every project.

Transparency — We build systems that make data visible, auditable, and trustworthy. Accountability — Our platforms embed responsibility into workflows, not afterthoughts. Inclusion — We design for farmers, SMEs, and citizens, not just institutions. Innovation — We use AI and data to solve problems traditional systems cannot. Integrity — We deliver what we promise and build partnerships based on trust. Impact — We measure success by economic growth, jobs created, and lives improved.
