Overview & Value Proposition

Affiliate & Partner Network Management Platform enables software providers, technology distributors, and enterprises to manage distributed sales networks. It handles unique referral link generation, conversion tracking, multi-tier commission calculations (10%–50%), marketing asset distribution, and automated partner payouts.

Business & Economic Impact

Turns customer word-of-mouth and reseller networks into a scalable sales channel, reducing customer acquisition costs (CAC) by up to 60%.

Key Operational Capabilities

Unique Referral Link & Campaign Code Generator
Real-Time Click, Lead, and Paid Customer Tracking
Multi-Tier Commission Engine (10% Influencers, 20-30% Resellers, 30-50% Partners)
Marketing Collateral & Promotional Kit Library
Automated Monthly Payout & Ledger Reconciliation
Partner Performance Leaderboards & Analytics
