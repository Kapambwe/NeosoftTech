Overview & Value Proposition

Core Banking Administration Platform is a multi-tenant cloud banking infrastructure engineered for financial institutions across Africa. It enables bank managers, risk officers, and lenders to manage credit scoring, automate group micro-loans, process insurance claims, and calculate pension payouts without complex on-premise IT setups.

Business & Economic Impact

Reduces branch deployment costs by 75%, automates micro-loan underwriting from days to seconds, and enforces regulatory compliance out-of-the-box.

Key Operational Capabilities

Dynamic Credit & Risk Scoring Engine
Automated Group Micro-Lending Workflows
Insurance Claims & Pension Payout Processing
Multi-Tenant Multi-Currency Account Ledgers
Embedded AML/CFT Screening & KYC Verification
Real-Time Central Bank Supervision Dashboards
