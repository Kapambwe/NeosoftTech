Digital Government

Every kwacha of public funds deserves an audit trail. We give councils, ministries, and regulators live dashboards that turn budget lines into verifiable outcomes — from procurement approvals to CDF project delivery.

SME & Investment

Africa's informal businesses are invisible to banks and investors without data. We transform small producers into bankable enterprises — with real-time performance scores, ETF-ready metrics, and AI-powered investment pipelines.

Agriculture & Food

Zambia's 3 million farmers are invisible to lenders, markets, and policy-makers. We connect them — capturing every crop cycle, extension visit, and market price into a data system that unlocks finance and food security.

Mining & Resources

Artisanal miners produce wealth but remain outside the formal economy. We formalise the value chain — from registration and production tracking to direct market access — so communities keep more of what they produce.

Financial Crime & Risk

Corruption follows patterns. Our AI-powered graph and network analysis platforms detect anomalies in procurement, transactions, and financial flows before they become losses — supporting ACC, DEC, and FIC investigations.

Land & Citizens

Land disputes cost economies billions in lost investment. We digitise customary land governance, national land audits, and citizen services — making every parcel accountable and every citizen visible to the state.

Education & Health

Schools and hospitals that don't track performance can't improve. We give head teachers, medical superintendents, and ministry directors the real-time operational intelligence they need to deliver better outcomes.

Accounting & Finance

Without reliable financial records, SMEs can't borrow and governments can't plan. We build the accounting infrastructure that transforms transactions into investment-grade data — the foundation for inclusive finance at scale.
