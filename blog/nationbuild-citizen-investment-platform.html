What Is NationBuild?

NationBuild is a citizen investment platform — a regulated, SEC Zambia-supervised digital marketplace where any Zambian with a verified identity and a mobile money account can invest in real, verified, asset-backed financial instruments. It was built for citizens who have never had access to a stock exchange, a bank investment account, or a government bond issuance — and who have been told, implicitly or explicitly, that investing is not for people like them.

It is for them. It is built specifically for them.

NationBuild connects to the National Digital Asset Token Registry — the verified record of Zambia's productive assets — and presents every approved token offering in a format that any citizen can understand, evaluate, and act on with K200 and a mobile money PIN. Every offering listed on NationBuild has been reviewed and approved by the Securities Commission of Zambia before it appears. No token reaches you without regulatory sign-off.

How to Start Investing — Six Steps from Zero to Portfolio

You do not need a bank account. You do not need a financial adviser. You do not need to go to a government office. You need a National Registration Card, a mobile phone, and K200. That is the entire barrier to entry on NationBuild.

1
📱

Download the NationBuild App and Register

NationBuild is available on any smartphone — Android or iPhone. Open the app, enter your NRC number, take a selfie, and your identity is verified against the National Digital Identity Registry in under 60 seconds. No bank account needed. No office visits. No paper forms.

2
✅

Complete Your Investor Profile

Answer five simple questions about your investment goals — how much you can invest each month, what types of returns you are seeking, and how comfortable you are with different levels of risk. NationBuild uses your answers to recommend the right token categories for your situation.

3
🔍

Browse and Choose Your Investments

The NationBuild marketplace shows you every SEC Zambia-approved token offering — government bonds, harvest tokens, copper mine royalties, SME revenue shares, and cooperative assets. Each listing shows the verified issuer, the expected return, the minimum investment, and the SEC Zambia approval reference. Read the verified prospectus for any offering before investing.

4
💸

Invest Using Mobile Money

Select the amount you want to invest and confirm the transaction using your Airtel Money or MTN MoMo PIN. The funds leave your mobile wallet and are held in SEC Zambia-mandated escrow until your tokens are issued. You receive a digital token in your NationBuild portfolio within minutes. You are now an investor.

5
📊

Track Your Portfolio and Receive Income

Your NationBuild dashboard shows every token you hold, its current market value, the income you have earned, and your complete investment history. When income is distributed — quarterly for most tokens — it arrives automatically in your mobile money wallet. You can reinvest it, send it to your family, or save it. Your money works for you.

6
🔄

Trade, Hold, or Grow

NationBuild tokens are tradeable on the secondary market. If you need cash, you can sell your tokens to another investor on the exchange — at the current market price, with real-time settlement. If you want to grow, you can reinvest your income automatically. Every decision is yours. No lock-in periods. No hidden fees.

In month eighteen he had K520 in tokens and K140 in income distributions credited to his Airtel wallet. He had never earned money while sleeping before. Is NationBuild Safe? — What the Regulation Means for You

Every token offering on NationBuild is approved by the Securities Commission of Zambia before it is listed. The SEC reviews the issuer's asset records, financial disclosures, and corporate governance before granting an approval reference number. Every investor on the platform is protected by the same regulatory rules that govern the Lusaka Securities Exchange.

  • ✓All token offerings reviewed and approved by SEC Zambia — no listing appears without a regulatory reference number
  • ✓All investor funds held in SEC-mandated escrow until tokens are issued — your money is never unprotected
  • ✓Real-time trade surveillance by SEC Zambia — price manipulation and fraud are detected and stopped immediately
  • ✓Your identity is linked to the National Digital Identity Registry — your account cannot be hijacked by a fraudster
  • ✓Circuit breakers prevent extreme price movements — your investment cannot lose 50% in a day on NationBuild
  • ✓Investor compensation fund maintained by the platform — regulated reserves available if the platform itself fails

Investing always carries risk. Returns on NationBuild are not guaranteed. The expected returns shown for each token category are projections based on the issuer's verified financial records — not promises. A government bond is the closest thing to a guaranteed return that exists in any investment market. Other token categories carry higher risk in exchange for higher potential returns. Invest only what you can afford to hold for the investment period. This article is for information only and is not personalised financial advice.
