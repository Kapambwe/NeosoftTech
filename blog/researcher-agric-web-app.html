Real-World Scenario: Extension Officer Mary & The Drought Crisis

The Operational Bottleneck: Extension Officer Mary manages over 800 smallholder maize farmers in Monze District during a severe mid-season drought spell. Her farmers rely on outdated crop recommendations printed 8 years ago. Meanwhile, at the ZARI research station 200km away, scientists have spent 3 seasons perfecting a high-yielding, short-duration drought-tolerant maize variety. But the trial results sit locked in disconnected Excel files awaiting statistical analysis, while advisory drafting takes months of institutional clearance.

The Digital Transformation: With the Agricultural Research Platform, ZARI researchers input real-time field trial observations on mobile devices. Built-in automated statistical engines (ANOVA, LSD variance tests) calculate crop performance instantly. Once validated, the variety is automatically registered in the National Variety Registry with regional performance maps.

Within minutes, Mary receives a structured, plain-language digital advisory on her phone specifically tailored to Southern Province soil conditions. She equips her farmer groups with exact planting windows, fertilizer rates, and drought mitigation steps—compressing a decade-long scientific lag into a 3-week operational deployment.

3 Strategic Capability Pillars

01

Automated Trial Analytics

Eliminating manual statistical computations. Researchers record field measurements digitally; ANOVA, LSD tests, and yield variance curves generate automatically, cutting analysis cycles from months to hours.

02

National Variety Registry

A unified digital catalog of officially released crop varieties mapped by agro-ecological zones, pest resistance profiles, and historical yield data under variable weather conditions.

03

IP & Extension Bridge

Converts validated research directly into structured extension briefs for field officers while tracking Plant Breeders Rights (PBR) and licensing royalties with commercial seed partners.
